# Business Recommendations

## Project: Lens Production Process Optimization

The project identified several opportunities to improve production efficiency and reduce manufacturing delays.

## 1. Rework Management

**Challenge:** Repeated rework increases production lead time and creates additional quality-control workload.

**Recommendations:**
- Monitor repeated rework cycles.
- Identify recurring defect patterns.
- Review whether repeated rework is technically and economically justified.

## 2. Handling and Transportation

**Challenge:** Scratches and chipping can occur during handling and movement between workstations.

**Recommendations:**
- Introduce protective surfaces and separators.
- Reduce unnecessary manual transfers.
- Standardise handling and transportation procedures.

## 3. Quality Control

**Challenge:** Waiting time at quality control contributes to overall production delays.

**Recommendations:**
- Monitor quality-control waiting times.
- Consider prioritising reworked molds.
- Review opportunities to combine inspection tasks.

## 4. Washing and Drying

**Challenge:** Salt and water marks contribute to quality-related rework.

**Recommendations:**
- Maintain clean water quality.
- Improve drying procedures.
- Reduce the time molds remain wet after processing.

## 5. Production Layout

**Challenge:** Movement between production areas and floors creates additional handling.

**Recommendations:**
- Review the positioning of frequently connected production stages.
- Reduce unnecessary movement between workstations.
- Consider planned washing-line relocation while maintaining required quality conditions.

## Expected Business Benefits

The recommendations aim to:

- Reduce avoidable rework.
- Improve production flow.
- Reduce unnecessary handling.
- Improve quality-control efficiency.
- Support shorter manufacturing lead times.

*These are proposed improvements from the consulting study, not measured post-implementation results.*
