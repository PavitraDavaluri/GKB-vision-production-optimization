# Analytical Approach

## Overview

This project used a mixed-methods research approach, combining Excel-based production data analysis with observations of manufacturing operations.

The objective was to understand factors contributing to production delays and identify opportunities for process improvement.

## 1. Data Collection

The team examined company-provided Excel reports covering:

- Production and packaging information
- Quality-control outcomes
- Rework records
- Manufacturing lead times

## 2. Data Preparation

Production and quality-control reports from multiple months were consolidated and reviewed to support analysis across the study period.

The datasets were examined separately where their units of observation differed.

## 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis focused on:

- Production lead-time patterns
- Frequency of rework cycles
- Relationship between rework and lead time
- Common quality defects
- Quality-control outcomes

## 4. Data Visualization

Tables and charts were used to identify trends, compare production outcomes and communicate findings.

## 5. Operational Research

The quantitative analysis was complemented by:

- Observations of manufacturing processes
- Discussions with production employees
- Examination of workflow and material movement
- Investigation of potential operational bottlenecks

## 6. Business Recommendations

The team translated its analysis into recommendations addressing:

- Repeated rework
- Quality-control waiting time
- Material handling
- Defect prevention
- Production workflow efficiency

## Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Interpretation
- Data Visualization
- Business Research
- Operational Analysis
