# Project Summary

## 1. Project Background

This project was undertaken as part of the International Consulting Bootcamp 2026, organised by Goa University and the University of Antwerp.

The study focused on understanding manufacturing operations and identifying opportunities to improve production efficiency.

## 2. Research Objective

The primary objective was to investigate production delays, understand the causes of rework and identify opportunities to reduce manufacturing lead time while maintaining quality standards.

## 3. Research Methodology

The project followed a mixed-methods case study approach.

**Quantitative Analysis**
- Examined production and packaging Excel reports.
- Analysed quality-control reports.
- Investigated production lead times.
- Examined rework frequency and defect patterns.

**Qualitative Research**
- Observed production-floor activities.
- Studied manufacturing workflows.
- Discussed operational challenges with employees.
- Examined material movement between production stages.

## 4. Key Areas Investigated

- Rework frequency and production delays.
- Quality-control waiting periods.
- Handling and transportation-related defects.
- Salt and water marks.
- Production layout and material movement.

## 5. Recommendations

The team developed recommendations focused on reducing avoidable rework, improving handling procedures, streamlining quality-control activities and optimising production flow.

## 6. Learning Outcomes

- Practical business data analysis.
- Understanding manufacturing operations.
- Identifying operational bottlenecks.
- Translating analytical findings into recommendations.
- Collaborative international consulting experience.
