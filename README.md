# GKB Vision – Lens Production Process Optimization

### International Consulting Bootcamp | 2026
**Goa University × University of Antwerp**

## Project Overview

This project focused on analysing and optimizing the production process of single-vision optical molds at GKB Vision.

The study aimed to understand production delays, investigate rework patterns, identify quality-related issues and develop practical recommendations to improve manufacturing efficiency.

## Business Challenge

The company aimed to reduce production lead time from approximately 10–11 calendar days to 5–6 calendar days for at least 90% of molds while maintaining quality standards.

## My Contributions

- Examined company-provided Excel datasets containing production, packaging and quality-control information.
- Analysed production lead times and rework frequency.
- Investigated the relationship between rework cycles and production delays.
- Examined recurring defects, including scratches, chipping, salt marks and water marks.
- Identified operational bottlenecks and potential sources of waiting time.
- Contributed to the final consulting report and management recommendations.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- Production lead-time analysis
- Rework and quality-control analysis
- Defect pattern identification
- Operational bottleneck investigation
- Production workflow and material movement

## Recommendations Developed

- Introduce structured monitoring of repeated rework.
- Improve protective handling and transportation procedures.
- Review quality-control workflow and prioritisation.
- Improve washing and drying procedures.
- Reduce unnecessary material movement between production areas.
